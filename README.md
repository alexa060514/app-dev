## Movie Description
Seven Sundays is a Filipino family drama about a father and his four children who reunite after learning about his serious illness. They spend seven Sundays together and face the problems that have affected their family for years. Through their time together, they learn to understand each other, forgive past mistakes, and value their remaining time as a family.
